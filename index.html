    How we calculate this

    Solar irradiance: we use NASA POWER satellite data (SSE-derived monthly irradiance averages) for your exact latitude and longitude — the same data backbone many commercial solar tools rely on.

    Australian adjustment: we apply a CER (Clean Energy Regulator) solar-zone factor based on your postcode. Australia is divided into 4 zones; lower zone numbers = stronger sun. This bakes in regional accuracy on top of the satellite data.

    System losses: 14% — the standard industry assumption covering inverter inefficiency, DC/AC conversion, cabling, dust, and minor shading.

    Panel orientation: we assume optimal tilt for your latitude with North-facing panels. Real installations are often constrained by roof angle and direction, so actual output may be a few percent below this estimate.

    Comparison data: typical home consumption averages from AEMC household energy reports, broken down by state.

    Fallback: if NASA POWER is unavailable, we use a peak-sun-hours formula by latitude, also adjusted by CER zone — slightly less precise but sensible.

    Estimate only. Australian context (CER zones, STC zones, state feed-in tariff ranges, consumption averages) is current as of 2026 and may change. Feed-in tariff ranges are indicative — actual rates vary by retailer and plan. For binding quotes, consult a licensed installer.
